# Personal Schedule Dashboard Template  
  
A customizable schedule and productivity dashboard that runs in your browser. Build your own weekly schedule, track daily habits, manage classes and assignments, and stay on task with a built-in study timer.  
  
[Live demo](https://lifeasjxss.github.io/Schedule-dashboard-template/)
  
## Features  
  
- **Custom weekly schedule:** Add your own time blocks for each day.  
- **Daily checklist:** Create your own recurring daily tasks.  
- **Weekly and monthly goals:** Set goals and track your progress.  
- **Daily check-in:** Write your own reflection questions and review your answers.  
- **Class and assignment tracker:** Add your classes, assignments, and due dates.  
- **Study timer:** Choose how long you want to study, and the timer counts down.  
- **Calendar view:** See upcoming due dates at a glance.  
  
## How to Use  
  
1. Open the live demo link above, or download this project and open `index.html` in your browser.  
2. Enter your name and set up your schedule, checklists, and classes.  
3. Come back any time. Your information is saved automatically.  
  
## Privacy  
  
All of your information is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server, and no one else can see it. Clearing your browser data will erase it.
